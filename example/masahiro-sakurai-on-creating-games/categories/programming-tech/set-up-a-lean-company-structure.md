# Set Up a Lean Company Structure

Category: Programming & Tech

## Summary
Small, flexible entities (like a yūgen gaisha) can work well for freelance developers.

## Use When
- Establishing your own development company.

## Do This
1. Consider simple corporate structure meeting legal needs without overhead. Keep team minimal, avoid permanent employees unless necessary.

## Checklist
- Research local business entity types
- Choose structure limiting liability and tax burden
- Keep admin costs low
- Outsource non-core tasks

## Anti-Patterns
- Over-hiring before revenue stable
- Complex structures with high compliance costs
- Mixing personal and business finances carelessly
