# The Fiend's Cauldron

Category: Design Specifics

## Summary
Risk/reward system in Kid Icarus: Uprising where players spend currency to raise difficulty (earn more) or can lower it. Dynamic balancing prevents hard locks.

## Use When
- Designing adjustable difficulty systems.

## Do This
1. Let players invest resources to increase difficulty/reward; ensure failure reduces difficulty but costs resources; communicate ability to lower.

## Checklist
- Offer player control over difficulty before stages
- Link reward to difficulty level
- Penalize failure with resource loss and auto-difficulty reduction
- Explicitly teach both increasing and decreasing options

## Anti-Patterns
- Difficulty only increasing
- No visible risk/reward trade-off
- Punishing failure too harshly
- Assuming players know they can lower
