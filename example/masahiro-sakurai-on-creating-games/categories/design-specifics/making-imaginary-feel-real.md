# Making the Imaginary Feel Real

Category: Design Specifics

## Summary
Virtual worlds become believable through small details: textures suggesting weight, wear-and-tear, and subtle environmental effects.

## Use When
- Creating 3D environments or objects that need to feel grounded.

## Do This
1. Add scratches, dirt, weathering; convey weight through appropriate animation; simulate airflow (e.g., grass swaying).

## Checklist
- Apply material textures suggesting surface properties
- Animate objects to imply mass
- Include environmental wind or particle effects
- Maintain consistency

## Anti-Patterns
- Objects too clean and untouched
- Lack of environment interaction
- Effects without visual feedback
