# Game Awards

Category: Design Specifics

## Summary
Game awards should celebrate diversity, not ranking; encourage awards highlighting originality.

## Use When
- Participating in or designing game awards.

## Do This
1. Understand awards' diversity value; support awards highlighting originality.

## Checklist
- Do awards make creators happy?
- Avoid overemphasizing ranking?
- Recognize commercial and original works?

## Anti-Patterns
- Treating awards purely as competition
- Ignoring niche and innovative works
