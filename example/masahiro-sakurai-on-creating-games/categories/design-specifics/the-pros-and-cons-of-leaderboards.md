# The Pros and Cons of Leaderboards

Category: Design Specifics

## Summary
Leaderboards give goals and matchmaking, but frustrate non-top players. Use inverted rankings (lower=better) and VIP rooms to soften blow.

## Use When
- Implementing competitive rankings or matchmaking.

## Do This
1. Consider inverted ranking system where lower numbers indicate higher skill. Create exclusive spaces for top players. Design matchmaking to balance wait time and skill gap.

## Checklist
- Ranking system chosen (traditional or inverted)
- VIP room or similar reward for top players
- Matchmaking parameters tuned
- Low-rank players have visible progress

## Anti-Patterns
- Only traditional leaderboards making most players feel bad
- Matching wide skill gaps
- No incentive to climb after certain rank
