# Making Lead-ins Instant and Impactful

Category: Design Specifics

## Summary
'Kamae' (wind-up) affects responsiveness and impact. Smash Bros. favors immediate pose change on input for quick response, but too little reduces impact. Balance based on online lag.

## Use When
- Designing attack animations.

## Do This
1. Test attacks with varying wind-up lengths. For responsive games, let character change pose immediately on input. Add subtle shake during hold to confirm charge.

## Checklist
- Wind-up clearly communicates incoming attack?
- Balance between responsiveness and impact?
- Tested with online latency?
- Instant attacks clearly telegraphed?

## Anti-Patterns
- Very long wind-ups feeling sluggish
- No wind-up causing weak impact
