# Visual Effects in Slow Motion

Category: Design Specifics

## Summary
Effects are often too fast to notice but greatly enhance atmosphere. Observing in slow motion reveals detail.

## Use When
- Designing or evaluating game effects.

## Do This
1. Use slow-motion previews to study effect details. Ensure effects match the action. Layer effects to enhance feel.

## Checklist
- Test effects with slow motion
- Compare with and without effects

## Anti-Patterns
- Overusing effects that obscure gameplay
- Neglecting effects entirely
