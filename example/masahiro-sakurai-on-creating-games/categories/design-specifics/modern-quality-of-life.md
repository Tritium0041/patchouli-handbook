# Modern Quality of Life Features

Category: Design Specifics

## Summary
The Last of Us Part 1 offers extensive accessibility and QoL settings: visual adjustments, controller remapping, subtitle customization, combat assists, and more.

## Use When
- Adding accessibility features or designing UI/UX for broad audiences.

## Do This
1. Provide presets for quick configuration. Allow full controller remapping including hold/tap distinctions. Include subtitle size, background, speaker direction. Offer navigation hints and combat assists.

## Checklist
- Colorblind-friendly options?
- Controller can be held in alternative ways?
- Subtitles readable?
- Combat simplified?
- Multiple rendering modes?

## Anti-Patterns
- Assuming all players use same control scheme or visual ability
