# Don't Anchor Your Center Point

Category: Design Specifics

## Summary
Avoid locking camera focal point rigidly to player character. Adjusting Y-axis delay during jumps or shifting forward during action enhances sense of height, speed, and feedback.

## Use When
- Designing camera systems for 3D games.

## Do This
1. Test focal point behavior during movement. For jumps, let character move vertically on screen while center point stays at original height. For action, offset slightly ahead.

## Checklist
- Character movement looks natural on screen?
- Jump height clearly visible?
- Speed and drift communicated by camera offset?
- Fixed center point used only where appropriate (e.g., racing)?

## Anti-Patterns
- Locking camera completely to character hiding height and dynamism
