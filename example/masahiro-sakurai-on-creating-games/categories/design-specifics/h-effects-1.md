# H: Effects 1 (Category Compilation)

Category: Design Specifics

## Summary
Covers color additive effects, explosion patterns, screen shake, hitmark design, hitstop behavior, directional hit indicators. Emphasizes combining clear geometric elements with additive layers.

## Use When
- Designing combat effects, explosions, or hit feedback.

## Do This
1. Use additive effects sparingly with clear shapes; follow explosion pattern: flash, smoke, dark smoke, dissipate; design hitmarks visible on any background.

## Checklist
- Combine additive and solid effects for clarity
- Explosion flash includes dark variation for bright backgrounds
- Vary screen shake intensity
- Hitmark: contrast, accuracy, diverse elements, not obscuring, proper duration
- During hitstop, continue hitmark movement for longer pauses

## Anti-Patterns
- Over-reliance on additive effects (fuzzy)
- Uniform screen shake
- Ignoring background brightness
