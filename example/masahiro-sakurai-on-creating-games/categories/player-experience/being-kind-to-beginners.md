# Being Kind to Beginners

Category: Player Experience

## Summary
When facing a clearly new player, tone down your skill level to give them a fun experience. Design your game to ease beginners in naturally.

## Use When
- Designing multiplayer for skill disparity.

## Do This
1. Match opponent's level; avoid showing off; let them learn by playing. Provide gentle difficulty curve, optional tutorials, adaptive AI.

## Checklist
- Detect skill level
- If disparity large, consider handicaps
- Celebrate beginner successes
- Design training modes that feel like play

## Anti-Patterns
- Smurfing where beginner can't learn
- Dumping all mechanics at once
- Making tutorials mandatory and boring
