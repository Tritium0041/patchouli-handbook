# Embracing Games of All Eras

Category: Player Experience

## Summary
Sakurai shares his childhood experiences and encourages players to explore older games to appreciate the medium's evolution.

## Use When
- Looking for new experiences or understanding game history.

## Do This
1. Seek out classic games from earlier generations. Play them with an open mind, understanding limitations of their time.

## Checklist
- List recommended classics from 80s and 90s
- Try to see what innovations each game brought
- Appreciate craft despite primitive graphics

## Anti-Patterns
- Dismissing old games as outdated
- Only playing modern AAA titles
