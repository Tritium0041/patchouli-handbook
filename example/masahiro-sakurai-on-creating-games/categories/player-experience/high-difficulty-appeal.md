# High Difficulty and Roguelike Appeal

Category: Player Experience

## Summary
High difficulty games thrive because players want to see what comes next. Simply copying difficulty without strong motivation leads to frustration.

## Use When
- Designing games with repeated failure or permadeath.

## Do This
1. Offer tantalizing glimpses of future content. Ensure each run feels different. Balance difficulty so player skill growth is noticeable.

## Checklist
- Players have clear short-term goals after failure
- Game reveals depth early to spark curiosity
- Difficulty ramp fair and skill-based
- Progress carries over

## Anti-Patterns
- Pure difficulty with no rewards
- Randomness that feels unfair
- Repeating same content with no variation
