# Say No to Slow!

Category: Player Experience

## Summary
Players universally dislike slowness: slow movement, long wait times, idle periods. Minimize all avoidable delays.

## Use When
- Polishing gameplay feel or addressing pacing complaints.

## Do This
1. Audit all wait times. Cut or accelerate anything that does not entertain. Use filler screens if load unavoidable.

## Checklist
- List areas where players wait
- Measure wait times vs. perceived wait
- Optimize code for loading, animations for brevity
- Consider skip options for repeated sequences

## Anti-Patterns
- Long unskippable cutscenes
- Slow movement by default without sprint options
