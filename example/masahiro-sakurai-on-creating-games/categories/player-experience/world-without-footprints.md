# A World Without Footprints: Spoiler-Free Experience

Category: Player Experience

## Summary
To maximize personal discovery and emotion, avoid all trailers, guides, and gameplay videos until after finishing the game.

## Use When
- Playing a game with fresh eyes.

## Do This
1. Mute or avoid trailers; refuse to look up walkthroughs; explore thoroughly; only seek external opinions after completion.

## Checklist
- Block all media related to game
- Choose single-player offline mode
- Resolve puzzles without guides
- Save discussions until after credits

## Anti-Patterns
- Reading reviews before playing
- Watching let's plays that spoil surprises
- Using guides at first hint of difficulty
