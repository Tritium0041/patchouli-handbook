# Fuel Motivation with Anticipation

Category: Player Experience

## Summary
High difficulty is acceptable if players have a strong desire to see what comes next.

## Use When
- Designing challenging sections.

## Do This
1. Pair difficulty with compelling story, exploration rewards, or new mechanics players are eager to experience.

## Checklist
- Set up intriguing goals or narrative hooks
- Reveal glimpses of future content
- Ensure challenge feels surmountable with practice
- Provide checkpoints

## Anti-Patterns
- Difficulty for its own sake without context
- Repeating same content without new incentives
- Punishing failure without hope of progression
