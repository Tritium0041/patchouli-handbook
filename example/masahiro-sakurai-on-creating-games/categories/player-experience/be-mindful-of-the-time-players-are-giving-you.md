# Be Mindful of the Time Players Are Giving You

Category: Player Experience

## Summary
Player time is a cost, not free. Avoid advertising long playtimes upfront; make the game so engaging that players willingly invest time.

## Use When
- Designing game length, marketing copy, or onboarding flow.

## Do This
1. Focus on core loop density; reduce wait times; let players start quickly; never emphasize '100+ hours' unless extremely compelling.

## Checklist
- Analyze ratio of active control to passive watching
- Remove unnecessary delays
- Ensure first 15 minutes captivating
- Design for natural session length

## Anti-Patterns
- Boasting about length
- Long unskippable intros
- Excessive loading
