# Death Relief

Category: Player Experience

## Summary
In competitive games, players may feel relief when defeated due to quick respawns. Design for both sides to have fun.

## Use When
- Designing multiplayer or competitive modes.

## Do This
1. Enable fast restart/respawn. Ensure losing can still be enjoyable through spectacle or learning. Don't make death overly personal.

## Checklist
- Provide quick respawn or rematch options
- Give losing players something to watch or learn
- Avoid long penalty animations
- Design for both winner and loser engaged

## Anti-Patterns
- Long respawn timers
- Making death overly punitive
- Forcing unskippable death sequences
