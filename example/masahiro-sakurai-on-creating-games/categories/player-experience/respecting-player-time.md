# Respecting Player Time

Category: Player Experience

## Summary
Games that boast hundreds of hours of content can backfire; time is a cost. Developers should respect player time by allowing skips, fast travel, efficient saving.

## Use When
- Choosing games to play or designing systems.

## Do This
1. As player, recognize your time valuable. As designer, allow players to finish in reasonable time and respect schedule.

## Checklist
- Include options to speed up or skip repetitive tasks
- Provide estimated playtime upfront
- Allow saving anywhere or frequent checkpoints

## Anti-Patterns
- Forcing players to grind for progress
- Punishing short sessions (no save)
- Marketing game solely on its length
