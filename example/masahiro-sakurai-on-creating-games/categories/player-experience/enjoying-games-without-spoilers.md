# Enjoying Games Without Spoilers

Category: Player Experience

## Summary
Sakurai prefers to walk on untouched snow: avoid any spoilers, walkthroughs, or let's plays.

## Use When
- Starting a new game for most impactful experience.

## Do This
1. Before playing, don't watch any trailers or read reviews. Play entirely blind.

## Checklist
- Mute keywords and avoid gaming forums
- Set aside uninterrupted time for first hours
- Resist urge to look up solutions

## Anti-Patterns
- Watching full playthrough before touching game
- Reading strategy guides on first playthrough
