# Branching Tastes

Category: Player Experience

## Summary
Player interests become more diverse with age. Younger players form a broad 'trunk' market; older ones split into niches.

## Use When
- Defining target audience for a game.

## Do This
1. Assess if game aims for broad (younger) or niche (older) audiences. Leverage age-based trends.

## Checklist
- Identify age range of target audience
- Research prevalent tastes in that demographic
- Align game difficulty with audience maturity

## Anti-Patterns
- Assuming all gamers have similar preferences
- Ignoring demographic data
