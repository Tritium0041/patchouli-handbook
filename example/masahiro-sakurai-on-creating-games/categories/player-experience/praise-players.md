# Praise Players Constantly

Category: Player Experience

## Summary
Use positive feedback to celebrate player actions, even for opponent's mistakes.

## Use When
- Designing feedback systems.

## Do This
1. Add celebratory effects like confetti, sound cues, reward pop-ups for successes and opponent errors.

## Checklist
- Identify moments that can be celebrated
- Design flashy but non-obstructive effects
- Ensure immediate satisfying feedback
- Include positive feedback for opponent errors

## Anti-Patterns
- Only negative feedback on failure
- Delayed or unclear feedback
- Overwhelming clutter
