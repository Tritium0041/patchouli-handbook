# Make Retries Quick

Category: Player Experience

## Summary
Minimize downtime after failure. Fast retries keep players engaged and increase tolerance for difficulty.

## Use When
- Designing failure states, checkpoints, or restart mechanics.

## Do This
1. Keep death animations short (1-2 seconds). Optimize loading. Provide brief safety period after respawn.

## Checklist
- Time from death to re-control: ideally under 3 seconds
- Player can restart without excessive menu navigation
- Respawn invincibility if necessary
- Retry frequency limited to preserve challenge

## Anti-Patterns
- Long unskippable death sequences
- Infinite retries without penalty
- Spawning directly into danger
