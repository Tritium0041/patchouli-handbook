# Kirby's Dream Land: Design Philosophy

Category: Game Concepts & Case Studies

## Summary
First Kirby game deliberately lowered difficulty to guide new players: flight avoids fall deaths, inhaling enemies reduces risk, simple controls.

## Use When
- Designing character abilities or difficulty curve.

## Do This
1. Give player ability to avoid fatal mistakes (flight, float). Turn enemies into resources. Simplify controls. Add fun details like clear dance.

## Checklist
- Auto death prevention (flight, double jump)?
- Players can use enemies to reduce risk?
- Intuitive controls?
- Difficulty gradient easy to hard?

## Anti-Patterns
- Deliberately high difficulty for core players
- Frequent death from falling
- Complex controls confusing newcomers
