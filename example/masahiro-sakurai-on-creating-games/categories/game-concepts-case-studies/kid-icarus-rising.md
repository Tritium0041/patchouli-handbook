# Kid Icarus: Uprising - Innovative Design

Category: Game Concepts & Case Studies

## Summary
A case study in hybrid genre (rail shooter + TPS) on 3DS, with touch-screen aiming, large enemies, full narratives in gameplay.

## Use When
- Conceptualizing game that blends genres or leverages unique hardware.

## Do This
1. Identify core innovation defining game. Combine genres naturally. Design levels showcasing both modes. Integrate story without interrupting flow. Balance weapon variety.

## Checklist
- Core gameplay hook clear and unique
- Genre fusion serves purpose
- Controls intuitive and exploit hardware strengths
- Content variety prevents repetition
- Development risks anticipated

## Anti-Patterns
- Mixing genres without unifying vision
- Forcing touch controls
- Overcomplicating story
- Ignoring platform limitations
