# Guidelines

Category: Game Concepts & Case Studies

## Summary
Guidelines unify but may restrict creativity. Always question their necessity.

## Use When
- Adhering to platform or franchise guidelines while designing.

## Do This
1. Understand guideline purpose, negotiate flexibility, prioritize product quality.

## Checklist
- List applicable guidelines
- Identify where guidelines conflict with creativity
- Seek exceptions when beneficial

## Anti-Patterns
- Blindly following guidelines without thought
- Ignoring guidelines ensuring essential quality
