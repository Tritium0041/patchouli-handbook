# Kirby Super Star: Co-op and Anthology

Category: Game Concepts & Case Studies

## Summary
Two-player master/slave system and anthology structure enhance variety and replay value.

## Use When
- Designing co-op or multiple content styles.

## Do This
1. Consider master/slave mode for non-main player freedom; use short episodes to avoid long playthrough.

## Checklist
- Master/slave system implemented?
- Unique abilities per character?
- Content split into independent modules?

## Anti-Patterns
- Forcing dual local co-op with camera issues
- Long single play causing fatigue
