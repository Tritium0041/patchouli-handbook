# Making Things Look Miniature

Category: Visual & Graphics

## Summary
To create a miniature effect, blur top/bottom (shallow depth of field), increase saturation, and reduce blue (remove air perspective).

## Use When
- Creating tilt-shift miniature look or avoiding unintended miniaturization.

## Do This
1. Blur upper and lower portions of image to simulate shallow depth of field. Increase color saturation and reduce blue to remove air perspective.

## Checklist
- Blur applied to top and bottom of frame
- Saturation increased
- Blue tint reduced if needed
- Large objects not over-blurred or over-saturated

## Anti-Patterns
- Strong depth of field and high saturation on large objects making them look toy-sized
- Forgetting to remove air perspective when aiming for miniature effect
