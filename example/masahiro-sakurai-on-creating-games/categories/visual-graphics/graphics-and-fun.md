# Graphics and Fun

Category: Visual & Graphics

## Summary
Good graphics enhance immersion and player engagement, especially in atmospheric games and online storefronts.

## Use When
- Deciding art direction investment.

## Do This
1. Match graphic detail to gameplay demands. Design backgrounds that convey gameplay-relevant info. Ensure storefront visuals attract target audience.

## Checklist
- Design backgrounds that convey gameplay info
- Ensure storefront visuals attract audience
- Balance frame rate and visual quality

## Anti-Patterns
- Prioritizing graphics over gameplay
- Over-detailing a speed game
