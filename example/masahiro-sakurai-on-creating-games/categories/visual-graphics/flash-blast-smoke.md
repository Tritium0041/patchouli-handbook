# Flash, Blast, and Smoke: Explosion Effects

Category: Visual & Graphics

## Summary
A classic explosion effect consists of three phases: a quick flash, a central blast, and smoke to fade out. Consider background color and screen shake.

## Use When
- Creating explosion visual effects.

## Do This
1. Start with brief bright flash (with black if background is white). Follow with core fireball with sparks. Transition to smoke that gradually disappears. Add screen shake.

## Checklist
- Flash visible against all backgrounds
- Blast has clear shape and center
- Smoke transition feels natural
- Ground explosions include shockwave/debris

## Anti-Patterns
- Just a single fireball that fades out
- Flash too long or invisible on white backgrounds
