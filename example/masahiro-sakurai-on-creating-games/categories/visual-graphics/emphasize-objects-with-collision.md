# Emphasize Objects with Collision

Category: Visual & Graphics

## Summary
Make collision-enabled areas visually prominent; use atmospheric perspective and reduced contrast for non-collision areas.

## Use When
- Designing 3D stages where players need to quickly discern walkable surfaces.

## Do This
1. Identify all collision surfaces. Enhance their contrast and saturation. Apply atmospheric perspective to background elements without collision.

## Checklist
- Distinguished collision from non-collision areas visually
- Used atmospheric perspective on backgrounds
- Maintained overall aesthetic while prioritizing clarity

## Anti-Patterns
- Making background too detailed and distracting
- Assuming 3D perspective automatically shows collision
