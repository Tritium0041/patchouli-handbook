# Character Silhouette and Readability

Category: Visual & Graphics

## Summary
Distinct silhouettes are crucial for character recognition in fast-paced games. Adjust characters to ensure their profile is immediately identifiable.

## Use When
- Designing character appearances, especially for action games.

## Do This
1. Silhouette-test your characters: fill them in black and see if they are distinguishable. Preserve iconic elements in profile.

## Checklist
- Check silhouettes from front, side, and 3/4 views
- Ensure accessories add to distinctiveness
- Adjust proportions to emphasize key features
- Test readability on small screens

## Anti-Patterns
- Making characters look similar in shape
- Adding too many small details that blur silhouette
- Ignoring color contrast for outline visibility
