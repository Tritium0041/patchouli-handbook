# Graphical Polish and Consistency

Category: Visual & Graphics

## Summary
Small adjustments to characters and environments elevate the final product. Pay attention to foot placement, color palettes, and animation direction.

## Use When
- Polishing character models, animations, and level art before release.

## Do This
1. Create a feedback loop where artists and directors review every frame. Prioritize consistency across all states. Fix small flaws that break immersion.

## Checklist
- Review all animations for alignment and weight
- Check color variants for contrast and faithfulness
- Ensure effects don't obscure characters
- Fix any clipping issues

## Anti-Patterns
- Rushing through the polish phase
- Accepting small flaws because 'players won't notice'
- Changing art direction late in development
