# F: Graphics 1 (Category Compilation)

Category: Visual & Graphics

## Summary
Compilation of graphics supervision tips: draw reflected light, emphasize hit-detection areas, allocate performance budget, adjust scale via texture/lighting, use Photoshop for quick mockups.

## Use When
- Creating in-game art or optimizing rendering.

## Do This
1. Analyze light direction before painting. Use high contrast on collision surfaces, mute non-interactive areas. Prioritize detail on center-screen characters. Use Photoshop for fast reference images.

## Checklist
- Light reflection painted, not material
- Hit areas visually distinct
- Performance budget focused on focal points
- Scale matched via texture and lighting
- Quick mockups with layer tools

## Anti-Patterns
- Painting material color ignoring light
- Uniform detail everywhere
- Wrong scale cues
