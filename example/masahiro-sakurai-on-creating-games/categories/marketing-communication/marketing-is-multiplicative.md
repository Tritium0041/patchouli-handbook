# Marketing is Multiplicative

Category: Marketing & Communication

## Summary
Development and marketing are like two wheels; both must work for success. Marketing multiplies the value of development.

## Use When
- Planning game announcements or boosting awareness.

## Do This
1. Treat marketing as part of development. Check official channels. Create standalone promotional content. Maintain steady but not excessive cadence.

## Checklist
- Allocate time for marketing activities
- Produce shareable assets
- Monitor social media feedback

## Anti-Patterns
- Ignoring marketing entirely
- Over-saturating with announcements
