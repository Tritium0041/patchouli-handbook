# Show Actual Gameplay

Category: Marketing & Communication

## Summary
Marketing material must show actual gameplay footage early. Avoid long logos, trailers, or intros.

## Use When
- Creating trailers, store pages, or promotional videos.

## Do This
1. Open video with snippet of exciting gameplay. Keep logo under 2 seconds. Let mechanics sell themselves.

## Checklist
- Does trailer start with gameplay within 3 seconds?
- Showing core rotation rather than cutscenes?
- Gameplay footage representative of final quality?

## Anti-Patterns
- Opening with CGI not representative
- Showing too much story setup
- Using only pre-rendered footage
