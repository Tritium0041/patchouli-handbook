# The Price of Games

Category: Marketing & Communication

## Summary
Games are cheap relative to other entertainment; support developers by buying games directly or playing them via subscription.

## Use When
- Thinking about pricing or advocating for game value.

## Do This
1. Recognize value of games compared to movies or dining. If you enjoy a game, purchase it.

## Checklist
- Acknowledged game costs have not risen proportionally
- Consider buying instead of only subscribing

## Anti-Patterns
- Expecting games cheap or free
- Relying solely on subscriptions without direct purchases
