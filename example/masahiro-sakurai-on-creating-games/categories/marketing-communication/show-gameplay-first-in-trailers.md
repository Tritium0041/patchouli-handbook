# Show Gameplay First in Trailers

Category: Marketing & Communication

## Summary
Begin trailers with actual game footage rather than logos or cinematic intros.

## Use When
- Editing any gameplay trailer.

## Do This
1. Open with exciting gameplay moment to hook viewers immediately; delay logos until after hook.

## Checklist
- Select most impressive gameplay clip
- Edit out long build-ups
- Ensure first five seconds gripping
- Place studio logos at end or brief

## Anti-Patterns
- Long logo animations at start
- Cinematic intro hiding gameplay
- Slow buildup before action
