# Sustaining Information Flow

Category: Marketing & Communication

## Summary
The 'Smash Bros Ticket' website kept fans engaged with daily updates. Continuous, fresh information builds loyal audience.

## Use When
- Launching game's official website or social media campaign.

## Do This
1. Plan content calendar releasing new info regularly. Use dedicated platform for fans. Keep tone consistent and informative.

## Checklist
- Decide update frequency
- Assign team member to manage content
- Create backlog of interesting facts and reveals
- Engage with community feedback

## Anti-Patterns
- Releasing all info at once
- Updating irregularly or going silent
- Using vague or misleading teasers
