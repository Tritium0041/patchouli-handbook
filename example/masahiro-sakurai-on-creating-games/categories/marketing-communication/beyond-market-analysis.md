# Beyond Market Analysis

Category: Marketing & Communication

## Summary
Market analysis relies on past data and cannot predict future hits. Sakurai advises a skeptical stance: use for reference, not as compass for innovation.

## Use When
- Planning new game concept or positioning in market.

## Do This
1. Consider market data as one input. Trust creative intuition and unique value propositions. Be prepared to walk lonely path.

## Checklist
- Gather data on trends
- Identify gaps or over-saturated areas
- Brainstorm ideas against data
- Test risky concepts with small prototypes

## Anti-Patterns
- Letting data dictate every design decision
- Dismissing creativity
- Assuming what worked before will work again
