# Produce Trailers Step-by-Step

Category: Marketing & Communication

## Summary
Trailer creation involves multiple iterations: storyboard, animation, CG, sound, and frequent reviews.

## Use When
- Creating any promotional video.

## Do This
1. Start with detailed storyboard, then animate key scenes, add effects and sound, iterate based on team feedback.

## Checklist
- Draft storyboards focusing on composition
- Produce rough animation to test timing
- Incorporate CG assistance for polish
- Add sound effects and music
- Review and refine every frame

## Anti-Patterns
- Skipping storyboard phase
- Ignoring sound design until end
- Rushing to meet deadline without quality checks
