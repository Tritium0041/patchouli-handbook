# Effective Presentation

Category: Marketing & Communication

## Summary
Demos and pitches must be fast and dense. Cut all fluff. Show the game early, summarize quickly, let gameplay speak.

## Use When
- Pitching to executives, press, or publishers.

## Do This
1. Outline talk, strip to essentials. Ensure each slide has single takeaway. Speak energetically; avoid reading slides.

## Checklist
- First slide gets into game within 10 seconds?
- Redundant slides eliminated?
- Timed under 5-10 minutes?

## Anti-Patterns
- Starting with studio history
- Bullet points without visuals
- Talking too slowly
