# Create Shareable Character Reveal Videos

Category: Marketing & Communication

## Summary
Design reveal trailers that are exciting, self-contained, and shareable without context from the main game.

## Use When
- Announcing new characters or major updates.

## Do This
1. Produce cinematic yet gameplay-driven trailers highlighting character personality and key moves.

## Checklist
- Focus on one character per video
- Show iconic moves
- Balance humor and epicness
- Avoid story spoilers

## Anti-Patterns
- Revealing too many characters at once
- Trailers requiring game context to understand
- Over-relying on CGI without gameplay
