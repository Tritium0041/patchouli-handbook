# Treat Marketing as Essential

Category: Marketing & Communication

## Summary
A great game must also be well-known; invest equally in development and promotion.

## Use When
- From early stages of game planning.

## Do This
1. Plan marketing strategies alongside development. Ensure game discoverable through appropriate channels.

## Checklist
- Allocate budget and time for marketing
- Identify target audience and platforms
- Create marketing calendar aligned with milestones
- Engage with community early

## Anti-Patterns
- Releasing without any promotion
- Assuming word-of-mouth alone
- Over-hyping before game ready
