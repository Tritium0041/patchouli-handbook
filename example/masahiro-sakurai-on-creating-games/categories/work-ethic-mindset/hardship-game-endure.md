# You'll Forget the Hardship, But the Game Will Endure!

Category: Work Ethic & Mindset

## Summary
Game development is hard, but the pain fades while the game remains. Remember that millions may play your game.

## Use When
- Feeling demotivated or questioning effort.

## Do This
1. Focus on long-term joy of players. Keep mental image of future player enjoying your work. Streamline processes, but never cut corners on essential quality.

## Checklist
- Remind yourself of game's potential reach
- Set realistic milestones to avoid burnout
- Celebrate small victories
- Prioritize features providing most player value

## Anti-Patterns
- Working overtime without purpose
- Sacrificing quality for speed
