# Creators Change, Too

Category: Work Ethic & Mindset

## Summary
Developers may leave the game industry but find success elsewhere. Don't assume they've failed; change is natural.

## Use When
- Reflecting on career longevity or seeing peers leave.

## Do This
1. Stay open to change in your own career. Support colleagues who choose different paths. Don't judge creators who go silent.

## Checklist
- Acknowledge change as part of growth
- Avoid labeling someone as 'finished' if they leave
- Consider value of diversifying experience

## Anti-Patterns
- Gossiping about absent creators as 'washed up'
- Resisting personal change
- Believing staying in games forever is superior
