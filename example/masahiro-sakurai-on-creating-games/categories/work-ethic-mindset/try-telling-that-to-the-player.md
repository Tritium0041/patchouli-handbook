# Try Telling That to the Player

Category: Work Ethic & Mindset

## Summary
Develop with player as priority; don't sacrifice player experience for development convenience.

## Use When
- Designing features that bias development convenience.

## Do This
1. View design from player perspective; collaborate with programmers to find alternatives.

## Checklist
- Is design player-centric?
- Sacrificing player experience for dev convenience?
- Explored different implementations?

## Anti-Patterns
- Specs only considering dev convenience
- Assuming players understand development difficulties
