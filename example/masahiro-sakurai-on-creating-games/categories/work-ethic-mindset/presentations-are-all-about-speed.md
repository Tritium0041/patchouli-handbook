# Presentations Are All About Speed

Category: Work Ethic & Mindset

## Summary
Demos and pitches must be fast and dense. Cut all fluff. Show the game early, summarize quickly.

## Use When
- Pitching to executives or press.

## Do This
1. Outline talk, then strip to essentials. Ensure each slide has single takeaway. Speak energetically; avoid reading slides.

## Checklist
- Does first slide get into game within 10 seconds?
- Are there redundant slides?
- Timed presentation under 5-10 min?

## Anti-Patterns
- Starting with studio history
- Bullet points without visuals
- Talking too slowly
