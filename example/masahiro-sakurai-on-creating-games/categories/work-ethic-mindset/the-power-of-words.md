# The Power of Words

Category: Work Ethic & Mindset

## Summary
Spoken words shape reality through influence on mindset and actions. Avoid negative talk; channel negativity into improvement.

## Use When
- During high-stress development or team morale dips.

## Do This
1. Catch yourself complaining and rephrase as constructive. Use encouraging language with team. When negative, write down specific issues to address.

## Checklist
- Identify common negative phrases in team communication
- Rephrase complaints into action items
- Celebrate small wins verbally
- Encourage team to express concerns without spiraling

## Anti-Patterns
- Frequent complaining demoralizing team
- Ignoring real problems because of 'positive thinking'
- Denying negative emotions instead of channeling them
