# Elementary School Play Testers

Category: Work Ethic & Mindset

## Summary
Invite children to playtest early builds for unfiltered feedback on engagement, difficulty, and fun.

## Use When
- Early development needing authentic feedback.

## Do This
1. Create casual party-like atmosphere. Observe without interfering. Note where they delight, struggle, lose interest.

## Checklist
- Arrange relaxed space
- Invite 3-5 children varied ages
- Assign observers to note reactions
- Avoid leading questions
- Use findings to adjust

## Anti-Patterns
- Treating as formal bug test
- Coaching children
- Ignoring emotional responses
