# Competition and Abundance in Game Development

Category: Work Ethic & Mindset

## Summary
The game industry is fiercely competitive, yet abundant with opportunities. Developers should persist, and players should appreciate the effort.

## Use When
- Feeling demotivated or questioning value of work.

## Do This
1. Acknowledge competition but embrace abundance. Find joy in creation. Thank other developers. View games as labor of love.

## Checklist
- Accept that many want to make games, but that's fine
- Focus on own creative journey
- Appreciate scale of effort
- Use competition as fuel

## Anti-Patterns
- Giving up because market seems saturated
- Disrespecting other developers' work
- Comparing too harshly to others
