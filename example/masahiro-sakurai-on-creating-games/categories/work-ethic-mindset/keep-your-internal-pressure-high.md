# Keep Your Internal Pressure High

Category: Work Ethic & Mindset

## Summary
Keep early ideas secret to build creative pressure, leading to more passionate and refined outputs.

## Use When
- Starting a new project or writing proposal.

## Do This
1. Work alone until complete draft ready. Avoid sharing progress prematurely.

## Checklist
- Set goal to finish first draft in isolation
- Resist urge to discuss or tweet
- Imagine audience reactions for motivation
- Only seek feedback after draft solid

## Anti-Patterns
- Brainstorming in groups too early
- Posting WIP on social media
