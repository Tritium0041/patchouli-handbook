# Directors Need to Be Unique

Category: Work Ethic & Mindset

## Summary
Great game directors share a trait: they notice nuances others miss and think differently. Cultivate your own perspective.

## Use When
- Leading a team or developing personal design style.

## Do This
1. Practice seeing ordinary as extraordinary. Document unique observations. In discussions, offer viewpoints that challenge assumptions.

## Checklist
- Daily: note one thing you noticed others might ignore
- Share perspective in team discussions
- Prototype mechanic based on unique insight
- Seek feedback to ensure ideas comprehensible

## Anti-Patterns
- Copying other directors' styles
- Being so eccentric communication breaks down
