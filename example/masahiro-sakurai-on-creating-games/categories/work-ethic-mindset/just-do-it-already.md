# Just Do It Already!

Category: Work Ethic & Mindset

## Summary
The hardest part is starting. Overcome procrastination by immediate action. Motivation follows action.

## Use When
- Feeling unmotivated to start a task.

## Do This
1. Start immediately without thinking. Count down from 5 and begin. Break task into tiny first step. Momentum builds.

## Checklist
- Have you committed to just first 5 minutes?
- Are you waiting for perfect moment?
- For habits, lower the bar (10 minutes daily)

## Anti-Patterns
- Overplanning and never executing
- Waiting for inspiration
- Blaming lack of time or tools
