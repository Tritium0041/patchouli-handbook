# Make Games Because You're Good At It

Category: Work Ethic & Mindset

## Summary
Make games because you're good at it, not just because you love them. Work is work; be reliable, respect constraints, focus on delivering a great player experience.

## Use When
- Starting a new project or feeling demotivated.

## Do This
1. Treat game making as a craft. Always consider player first. When asked to create outside your preference, find value in challenge.

## Checklist
- Are you prioritizing player enjoyment over own tastes?
- Can you explain motivation beyond 'I love games'?
- Meeting deadlines and respecting team?

## Anti-Patterns
- Ignoring market needs because 'I want my dream game'
- Dismissing feedback because 'player will understand'
- Working sporadically based on mood
