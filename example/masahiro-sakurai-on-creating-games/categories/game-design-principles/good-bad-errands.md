# Good Errands and Bad Errands

Category: Game Design Principles

## Summary
Fetch quests can be satisfying if designed well: appropriate rewards, small incentives along the way, multiple strategies, quick return, compelling story.

## Use When
- Designing side quests or mission structure.

## Do This
1. Place reward caches on the path. Let players choose routes. Use fast travel after completion. Weave into narrative.

## Checklist
- Clear reward for completion
- Intermediate rewards along route
- At least two completion methods
- Shortcut or quick return
- Story motivation

## Anti-Patterns
- Sending players across map without fast travel
- Repeating same fetch task multiple times
