# Player Guidance and Freedom

Category: Game Design Principles

## Summary
Decide how much to guide the player. Explicit instructions help weaker players but reduce freedom. More open designs encourage exploration but may lead to confusion.

## Use When
- Designing tutorials, boss fights, or progression paths.

## Do This
1. For mainstream games, provide clear hints for critical puzzles; for hardcore audiences, allow discovery. Always let the player know what they can do at any moment.

## Checklist
- Identify target audience's comfort with ambiguity
- For compulsory challenges, give explicit clues
- For optional challenges, hide solutions in the environment
- Test with inexperienced players

## Anti-Patterns
- Forcing a specific strategy without alternatives
- Assuming all players enjoy the same level of guidance
- Making the first hour a tutorial that never lets go
