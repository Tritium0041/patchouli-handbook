# Difficulty Balance and Player Agency

Category: Game Design Principles

## Summary
Games are most fun at the edge of win/lose, but being unable to complete is frustrating. Provide difficulty options, skip, or scaling to avoid abandonment.

## Use When
- Setting game difficulty levels.

## Do This
1. Include adjustable difficulty that can be changed mid-game. Offer skip options after several failures. Consider adaptive difficulty.

## Checklist
- Player can reduce difficulty without restarting
- Skip option after repeated failures
- No permanent soft-lock
- Hard mode still fair

## Anti-Patterns
- No difficulty option
- Difficulty spikes that feel arbitrary
- Adaptive difficulty that punishes skilled players
