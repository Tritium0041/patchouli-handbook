# Difficulty and Player Retention

Category: Game Design Principles

## Summary
Games are most exciting on the edge of victory and defeat, but excessive difficulty can drive players away. Provide non-humiliating escape routes like difficulty adjustment or skip options.

## Use When
- Designing game difficulty, especially for action games.

## Do This
1. Include options to lower difficulty after repeated failures, allow skipping tough sections, ensure the game remains completable without extreme frustration.

## Checklist
- Identify points where players might get stuck
- Add a 'continue' or 'retry' mechanism with score penalty rather than restart
- Offer difficulty settings that can be changed mid-game
- Test with average players

## Anti-Patterns
- Designing for an elite audience only
- Making the game harder because the developer found it easy
- Forcing players to replay large sections upon failure
