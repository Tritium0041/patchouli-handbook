# Fetch Quest Design

Category: Game Design Principles

## Summary
Good fetch quests satisfy five conditions: appropriate reward, short gratification spans, freedom of approach, quick extraction, and anticipation of future benefit.

## Use When
- Designing side quests or collectibles.

## Do This
1. Ensure each step grants a sense of progress. Let players choose how to complete. Allow quick return. Hint at bigger reward ahead.

## Checklist
- Reward matches effort
- Player feels reward every few minutes
- Different strategies possible
- Quick exit after completion
- Quest leaves a promise

## Anti-Patterns
- Long travel with no checkpoints
- Reward meaningless
- Backtrack through empty areas
- No hook for future content
