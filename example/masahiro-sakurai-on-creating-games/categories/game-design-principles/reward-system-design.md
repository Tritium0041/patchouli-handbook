# Reward System Design

Category: Game Design Principles

## Summary
Make rewards visible and desirable. Show progress bars (XP to next level) to motivate players. Use clear, immediate feedback for every action.

## Use When
- Designing progression systems or feedback loops.

## Do This
1. Display concrete metrics like experience bar. Reward frequently, even for small accomplishments. Use anticipation.

## Checklist
- Is the reward clearly communicated?
- Does the player know what to do next?
- Are rewards spaced to avoid boredom?

## Anti-Patterns
- Hiding progression
- Rewarding too rarely
- Rewarding so often it becomes meaningless
