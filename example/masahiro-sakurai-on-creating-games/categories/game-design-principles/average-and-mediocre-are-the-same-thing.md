# Average and Mediocre Are the Same Thing

Category: Game Design Principles

## Summary
Over-balancing leads to homogenization. In Smash, characters have distinct strengths and weaknesses with win rates ranging only from 47% to 51% across 87 characters—proving individuality and balance can coexist.

## Use When
- When balancing multiple characters or options.

## Do This
1. Embrace character diversity: amplify strengths and weaknesses rather than flatten them. Use win rate data to ensure fairness.

## Checklist
- Define each character's unique traits
- Monitor win rates
- Avoid reducing gaps too drastically
- Communicate balance philosophy

## Anti-Patterns
- Making all characters feel the same
- Ignoring competitive data
