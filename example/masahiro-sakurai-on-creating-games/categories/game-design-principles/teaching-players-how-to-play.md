# Teaching Players How to Play

Category: Game Design Principles

## Summary
Avoid long text tutorials and boring practice fields. Embed learning into gameplay via initial levels with contextual prompts.

## Use When
- Designing first levels or tutorial sequences.

## Do This
1. Introduce mechanics one at a time in a safe environment. Use contextual text prompts that fade. Let players immediately practice.

## Checklist
- Tutorial integrates with gameplay
- New mechanics taught one at a time
- Players can practice right after learning
- Practice mode is engaging
- Skip option for experienced players

## Anti-Patterns
- Wall of text before gameplay
- Boring empty room for practice
- Tutorial that can't be skipped or replayed
- Information overload
