# Mandatory Strategies and Strategic Balance

Category: Game Design Principles

## Summary
Nintendo often designs boss fights with clear mandatory strategies. This provides focus but can feel tutorial-like. Balance forced guidance with player autonomy.

## Use When
- Designing boss encounters or major challenges.

## Do This
1. Decide the desired level of player freedom. For forced strategies, ensure they feel intuitive. For freeform, ensure multiple viable strategies.

## Checklist
- Define intended player experience
- Design boss patterns that allow or restrict multiple solutions
- Test with players
- Iterate

## Anti-Patterns
- Overly scripted paths that remove all agency
- Freeform design that lacks cohesive challenge
