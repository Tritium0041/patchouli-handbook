# Design Rewards with Consumption

Category: Game Design Principles

## Summary
Pair rewards with meaningful consumption mechanics to maintain player motivation.

## Use When
- Creating progression and economy systems.

## Do This
1. Let rewards be spent or consumed for upgrades, unlocking content, or survival, creating a satisfying loop.

## Checklist
- Determine what players earn
- Design outlets for spending
- Ensure rewards feel valuable
- Balance income vs. expenditure

## Anti-Patterns
- Rewards with no purpose
- Hoarding without meaningful spending
- Forcing consumption that feels punitive
