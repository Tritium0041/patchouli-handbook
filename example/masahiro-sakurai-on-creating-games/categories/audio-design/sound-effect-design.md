# Sound Effect Design: Attack and Reverb Control

Category: Audio Design

## Summary
For impactful sound effects, ensure the attack (initial peak) is strong and prominent, and avoid excessive reverb that muddies subsequent hits.

## Use When
- Designing hit sound effects.

## Do This
1. Make the first frame of the sound the loudest (strong attack). Keep reverb short so overlapping sounds remain distinct.

## Checklist
- Attack peak occurs at beginning
- Reverb tail short enough to avoid smearing
- Test in multiplayer scenario

## Anti-Patterns
- Slow attack delaying perceived hit timing
- Long reverb creating muddy wall
