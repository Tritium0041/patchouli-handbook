# Voice Recording

Category: Audio Design

## Summary
Keep voice lines short and avoid excessive chatter during gameplay. Localize voices per region.

## Use When
- Recording character voices.

## Do This
1. Write concise lines. Record in a single session per character (about 1 hour). Give clear direction to voice actors.

## Checklist
- Write scripts with short lines, especially for battle cries
- Schedule focused recording session with limited retakes
- Provide context to voice actor

## Anti-Patterns
- Overly long monologues during gameplay
- Making characters talk constantly
