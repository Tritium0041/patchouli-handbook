# Music Production Flow

Category: Audio Design

## Summary
When commissioning music, let composers choose tracks they are passionate about, but supervise to ensure melody identity is preserved.

## Use When
- Outsourcing music composition.

## Do This
1. First select source music. Brief composers on context, BPM, target length (e.g., 2:30), loop points. Let them pick tracks. Review all scores and provide feedback.

## Checklist
- Compile a list of tracks from game's franchise
- Write comprehensive music brief per track
- Hold meeting with composers to discuss direction
- Review scores and give notes on melody faithfulness, BPM, dynamics

## Anti-Patterns
- Forcing composers to work on tracks they dislike
- Changing melody too much
