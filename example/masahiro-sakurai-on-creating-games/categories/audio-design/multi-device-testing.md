# Multi-Device Audio Testing

Category: Audio Design

## Summary
Test audio on a range of devices from high-end headphones to cheap TV speakers to ensure it sounds good everywhere.

## Use When
- Finalizing audio mix.

## Do This
1. Listen to your game through at least three output types: studio headphones, PC speakers, and low-end TV speakers. Adjust EQ and levels.

## Checklist
- Gather representative devices
- Note which frequencies cause distortion on bad speakers
- Adjust mix to avoid harsh highs or muddy lows

## Anti-Patterns
- Only testing on high-end equipment
- Ignoring mobile or Switch built-in speakers
