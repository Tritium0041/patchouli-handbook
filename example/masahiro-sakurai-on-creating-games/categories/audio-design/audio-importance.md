# Audio Importance

Category: Audio Design

## Summary
No masterpiece has bad audio. Sound effects and music are as critical as graphics and gameplay; they are what players remember most.

## Use When
- Budgeting time or resources for audio.

## Do This
1. Invest in high-quality sound design and music. Never treat audio as an afterthought.

## Checklist
- Allocate budget for professional sound design and composer
- Playtest with sound on; if players turn it down, it's a red flag

## Anti-Patterns
- Relying only on free sound libraries
- Leaving audio to the last minute
