# Famicom and NES Audio

Category: Audio Design

## Summary
Overview of Famicom/NES sound hardware: two pulse waves, one triangle, one noise, one DPCM. Later disk system added waveform memory; expansion chips caused regional differences.

## Use When
- Studying retro game audio or composing chipmusic.

## Do This
1. Learn each channel: pulse for melody, triangle for bass, noise for percussion, DPCM for samples. Experiment with duty cycles.

## Checklist
- Identify five basic channels
- Understand duty cycle variation
- Recognize DPCM as sampling channel
- Know that Japanese cartridges could have extra audio chips

## Anti-Patterns
- Overusing DPCM due to limited memory
- Forgetting that NES lacks expansion audio terminal
