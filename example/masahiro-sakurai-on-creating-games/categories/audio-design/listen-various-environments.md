# Listen in Various Environments

Category: Audio Design

## Summary
Always test audio on multiple devices: headphones, cheap TV speakers, PC speakers. Avoid changing reference headphones once set.

## Use When
- Mixing and mastering game audio.

## Do This
1. Test sound on at least three output devices. Adjust levels so important sounds cut through low-quality speakers.

## Checklist
- Audio clear on cheap TV speakers?
- Bass-heavy elements audible on small speakers?
- Haven't switched headphones recently?
- Tested both stereo and surround setups?

## Anti-Patterns
- Mixing only on high-end studio monitors
- Changing headphones mid-project
