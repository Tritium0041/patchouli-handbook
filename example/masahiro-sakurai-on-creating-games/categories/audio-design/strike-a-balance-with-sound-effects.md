# Strike a Balance with Sound Effects

Category: Audio Design

## Summary
Prioritize sound effect volume based on gameplay impact: K.O. sounds loudest, then hit sounds, then special moves, then movement.

## Use When
- Creating or balancing sound effect volumes.

## Do This
1. List all sound events by importance. Set K.O. highest. Hit sounds clear. Use randomization for repetitive sounds.

## Checklist
- Identified highest-priority sound
- Hit sounds louder than normal actions
- Added randomization to footsteps
- Considered character material for landing sounds

## Anti-Patterns
- All sounds equal volume
- Mechanically repeating same sound for steps
- Ignoring gameplay impact
