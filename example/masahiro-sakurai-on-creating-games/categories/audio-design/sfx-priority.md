# SFX Priority

Category: Audio Design

## Summary
Set volume levels for sound effects based on gameplay importance: K.O. sounds should be loudest, walking softest.

## Use When
- Mixing audio in a game.

## Do This
1. Establish a hierarchy: K.O. > hit > special move > voice > explosion > evade > whiff > dash > jump/land > walk. Adjust volumes accordingly.

## Checklist
- List all SFX categories
- Assign relative volume levels (e.g., 0 dB to -20 dB)
- Test in loud and quiet environments

## Anti-Patterns
- Making all sounds equally loud
- Cluttering with unnecessary ambient sounds
