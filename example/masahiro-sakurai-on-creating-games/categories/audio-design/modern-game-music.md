# Modern Game Music

Category: Audio Design

## Summary
With complex modern graphics, music should take a backseat; use ambient sounds and subtle variations of a main theme to create atmosphere.

## Use When
- Composing music for a visually rich game.

## Do This
1. Keep music volume moderate and layer with environment sounds. Use leitmotifs that change with situations.

## Checklist
- Design music to have dynamic layers
- Balance music vs SFX vs dialogue levels
- Ensure theme memorable but not intrusive

## Anti-Patterns
- Blasting music that drowns out gameplay sounds
- Making music too repetitive
