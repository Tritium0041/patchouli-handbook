# Falling Sells the Feeling of Flight

Category: Game Essence

## Summary
To make flying feel exhilarating, introduce the risk of falling (or other penalties). Players appreciate freedom more when there is danger.

## Use When
- Designing flight or high-speed traversal systems.

## Do This
1. Add a clear hazard for failure (e.g., ground collision). Design segments that force risk negotiation for rewards.

## Checklist
- Introduce consequence for failure
- Create specific risk zones
- Ensure successful evasion provides positive feedback
- Balance challenge

## Anti-Patterns
- Unrestricted flight without consequences
- Risks too punishing without reward
