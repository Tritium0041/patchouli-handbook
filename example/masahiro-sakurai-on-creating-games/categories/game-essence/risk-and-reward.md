# Risk and Reward: The Core of Game Essence

Category: Game Essence

## Summary
Game essence is defined by the trade-off between risk and reward. Classic examples like Space Invaders and Super Mario show how proximity to danger increases both tension and payoff.

## Use When
- Designing core mechanics, enemy patterns, or player abilities.

## Do This
1. Clearly define the risk and reward for each action; couple them tightly (e.g., close-range attacks dealing more damage) to create excitement.

## Checklist
- Identify the risk players must take
- Ensure the reward is proportional to the risk
- Balance risk timing to avoid frustration
- Consider the distance between player and threat

## Anti-Patterns
- Risk without any reward feels punishing
- Reward without risk becomes boring
- Overly complex risk calculations confuse players
