# Game Essence in Shooting Games

Category: Game Essence

## Summary
Risk-reward in shooters: positioning near enemies yields higher rewards; understand why a mechanic is fun, not just imitate.

## Use When
- Designing shooting mechanics.

## Do This
1. Study risk-reward trade-offs in positioning and weapon choice. Create intentional dilemmas.

## Checklist
- Map risk to reward
- Design components that force choices
- Test player strategies

## Anti-Patterns
- Mindless enemy waves without risk consideration
- Copying mechanics without understanding their appeal
