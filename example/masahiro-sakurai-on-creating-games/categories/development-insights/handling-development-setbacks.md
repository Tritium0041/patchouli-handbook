# Handling Development Setbacks

Category: Development Insights

## Summary
Game cancellations and delays happen. Sakurai emphasizes learning from failure, knowing when to cut losses, and maintaining team morale.

## Use When
- Facing project pressure, budget cuts, or creative blocks.

## Do This
1. Assess project viability early. If a concept isn't working, pivot or cancel with clear reasoning. Communicate transparently.

## Checklist
- Identify key risks and milestones
- Have contingency plan for major failures
- Review progress regularly: if core is broken, consider stopping
- Support team emotionally after cancellation

## Anti-Patterns
- Pushing forward with flawed concept out of pride
- Hiding problems until it's too late
- Blaming individuals rather than analyzing systemic issues
