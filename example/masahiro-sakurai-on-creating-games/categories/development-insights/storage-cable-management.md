# Storage and Cable Management

Category: Development Insights

## Summary
Organize game collections and development hardware with custom cabinets, cable management, and label printers.

## Use When
- Setting up a dev lab or organizing personal collection.

## Do This
1. Use custom shelves with ventilation. Centralize power strips with switches. Use cable boxes and ties. Label everything.

## Checklist
- All consoles have dedicated space with airflow
- Cables tidy and labeled
- Easy access to power switches
- Games sorted in binders or labeled boxes
- Scales with collection

## Anti-Patterns
- Tangled cables
- Overheating due to closed cabinets
- Cheap sleeves scratching discs
- Controllers out gathering dust
