# Role of the Director

Category: Development Insights

## Summary
Sakurai describes his role in Smash Bros: providing vision, solving key design hurdles, and personally playtesting. A director must be hands-on and make final decisions.

## Use When
- Leading a game project or defining responsibilities.

## Do This
1. Stay involved in critical aspects like core mechanics and balance. Encourage open communication. Be the final arbiter of design.

## Checklist
- Define game's vision and core pillars
- Personally test the game regularly
- Delegate but keep oversight on essential features
- Be willing to overrule opinions for the sake of product

## Anti-Patterns
- Being absent from day-to-day development
- Micromanaging every small detail
- Avoiding difficult decisions to please everyone
