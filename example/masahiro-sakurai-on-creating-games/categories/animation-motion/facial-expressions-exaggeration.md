# Facial Expressions Exaggeration

Category: Animation & Motion

## Summary
Facial expressions need to be stronger than you think; show upper and lower whites of eyes, avoid closed teeth, exaggerate mouth shapes.

## Use When
- Adding facial animations to characters.

## Do This
1. Widen eyes to reveal full iris and white above/below. Keep jaw slightly open. Push eyebrows and mouth more than realistic.

## Checklist
- Whites of eyes visible both above and below iris
- Teeth not completely closed (small gap)
- Expression conveys intended emotion clearly

## Anti-Patterns
- Subtle expressions lost in gameplay
- Eyes closed or squinting in surprise
