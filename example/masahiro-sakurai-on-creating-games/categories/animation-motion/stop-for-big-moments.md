# Stop for Big Moments

Category: Animation & Motion

## Summary
Hit stop and boss stop enhance impact. Early games already used these techniques. Apply to various genres.

## Use When
- Designing hit feedback or defeating bosses.

## Do This
1. Brief pause on attack hit; pause when defeating boss; adjust duration to avoid affecting flow; ensure effect not affected by frame rate.

## Checklist
- Clear hit feedback with pause
- Boss defeat has dramatic pause
- Pause duration refined
- Low frame rate still works

## Anti-Patterns
- Pause too long causing lag
- Missing stop feedback making hits weak
