# Rotation on Two Axes

Category: Animation & Motion

## Summary
Single-axis rotation looks robotic. Always combine at least two axes and stagger their timing. Add anticipation and adjust speed curves.

## Use When
- Animating any rotating body part to avoid stiffness.

## Do This
1. For head turns: combine Y (horizontal) with X (nod) or Z (tilt); for wrists: add tilt before rotation; offset timing; use ease curves.

## Checklist
- Select at least two rotation axes
- Offset keyframes between axes
- Adjust speed curves per axis
- Add anticipation if appropriate
- Align eye direction with head rotation

## Anti-Patterns
- Pure single-axis rotation
- All axes hitting keyframes simultaneously
- Ignoring line of sight
