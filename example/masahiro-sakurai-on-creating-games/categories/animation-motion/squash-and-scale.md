# Squashing and Scaling in Animation

Category: Animation & Motion

## Summary
Apply squash and stretch to add weight and energy. Maintain volume conservation. For extra quality, combine with blend shapes or mesh deformation.

## Use When
- Animating actions like jumping, landing, punching.

## Do This
1. Use scaling to exaggerate impact. Preserve overall volume: when scaling Y down, scale X up proportionally. For organic feel, use blend shapes.

## Checklist
- Squash and stretch used at key poses (anticipation, impact, follow-through)
- Volume appears roughly conserved
- Simple scaling supplemented with deformation for high-quality
- Not overused

## Anti-Patterns
- Uniform scaling without volume preservation
- Over-squashing causing visible distortion
- Using scaling only and ignoring bone/skin deformation
