# Breaking Down Attack Animations

Category: Animation & Motion

## Summary
Frame attack animation into four phases: idle, start-up, attack, follow-through. Emphasize strong first frame, hit stop on contact, and cancel frames in follow-through.

## Use When
- Authoring attack animations.

## Do This
1. Clearly communicate four frame types: idle pose, attack pose, active frames, total frames (including cancel point).

## Checklist
- Idle pose neutral and clear
- Start-up frame has large, immediate pose change
- Attack pose held during active frames (with hit stop)
- Follow-through includes cancel frames before full recovery
- Provide animators with precise frame data

## Anti-Patterns
- Slow start-up without visual telegraph
- No hit stop
- Recovery too long without cancel options
- Pose transitions too smooth losing impact
