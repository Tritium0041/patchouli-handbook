# Attack Poses

Category: Animation & Motion

## Summary
In action games, the pose during hitstop is critical for feel. Make poses exaggerated: cross the body's midline, avoid symmetry, twist torso, clear silhouettes.

## Use When
- Animating combat moves or refining hit impact.

## Do This
1. Design poses that break symmetry – one side extended, the other pulled back. Keep arms and legs clear of body silhouette. Have hitstop occur mid-motion.

## Checklist
- Animate clear, readable silhouette
- Cross the vertical centerline with attacking limb
- Avoid mirrored left/right poses
- Include slight torso twist
- Place hitstop at moment of impact

## Anti-Patterns
- Symmetrical poses that look static
- Hitstop at end of animation
