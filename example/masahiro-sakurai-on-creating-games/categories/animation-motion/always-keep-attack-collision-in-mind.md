# Always Keep Attack Collision in Mind

Category: Animation & Motion

## Summary
Attack collision areas are often larger than visual models to ensure hits connect. Animators must design motions with collision in mind.

## Use When
- Designing attack animations for action games.

## Do This
1. Ensure attack paths pass through collision zones. Use hitstop for decisive blows. Coordinate with planners to match animations with collision data.

## Checklist
- Check collision placement relative to animation keyframes
- Verify attack path intersects collision area
- Incorporate hitstop for final hits
- Review silhouette readability

## Anti-Patterns
- Collision too small causing frustration
- Animation that doesn't visually communicate attack range
