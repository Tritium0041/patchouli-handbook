# Establishing Characters Through Their Design

Category: Animation & Motion

## Summary
Character design should prioritize silhouette recognizability, exaggeration of key features, and consistent poses to convey personality.

## Use When
- Designing or approving character models.

## Do This
1. Ensure silhouette distinct even in black; exaggerate proportions that define character; use poses that express character traits; iterate with team.

## Checklist
- Silhouette test: can you identify character by outline?
- Exaggerate signature features
- Define default stance reflecting personality
- Seek feedback from others

## Anti-Patterns
- Overly realistic proportions
- Muddy silhouette
- Poses that hide character identity
