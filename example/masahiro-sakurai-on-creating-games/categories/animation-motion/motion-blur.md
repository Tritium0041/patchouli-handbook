# Motion Blur

Category: Animation & Motion

## Summary
Motion blur enhances speed feel but can reduce clarity. Use sparingly; provide intensity slider in options. Prefer artistic blur or hardware bloom.

## Use When
- Adding visual polish to fast-moving objects or camera.

## Do This
1. Apply blur only during fast motion. Keep blur short. Offer option to reduce/disable it.

## Checklist
- Blur improves sense of speed without obscuring details
- Blur intensity adjustable
- Blur works well with target frame rate

## Anti-Patterns
- Full-screen blur during gameplay
- Blur on UI elements
- Overuse causing motion sickness
