# Center of Gravity Shift

Category: Animation & Motion

## Summary
Offset the rotation pivot of an object from its geometric center to make movement feel organic and lively.

## Use When
- Animating any rotating or moving object.

## Do This
1. Add a small offset to pivot point. For sprites, cycle display position in a loop to simulate wobble.

## Checklist
- Identify objects that move in straight line or rotate evenly
- Apply offset or position cycling
- Test if movement feels more alive

## Anti-Patterns
- Always using geometric center as pivot
- Too symmetrical and boring
