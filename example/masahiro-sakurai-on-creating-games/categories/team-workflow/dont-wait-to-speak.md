# Don't Wait to Speak Your Mind

Category: Team Management & Workflow

## Summary
Express dissatisfaction or improvement ideas immediately within the team, not after completion. Pair criticism with solution. Establish 'Opinion Officer' role.

## Use When
- Working in team and noticing issues.

## Do This
1. Share concerns as soon as they arise, along with at least one alternative. If you have suggestion, put it on table.

## Checklist
- Have I voiced this concern within team?
- Have I proposed concrete improvement?
- Is there safe channel for feedback?
- Am I listening openly?

## Anti-Patterns
- Complaining only after project done
- Criticizing without offering fix
