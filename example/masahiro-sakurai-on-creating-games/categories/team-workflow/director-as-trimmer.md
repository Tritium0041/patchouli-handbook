# Director as Trimmer

Category: Team Management & Workflow

## Summary
A director's job includes cutting unnecessary or overly risky tasks to keep the project on track.

## Use When
- Managing scope and resources.

## Do This
1. Continuously evaluate project health. Cut features that are too heavy or likely to fail. Communicate cuts early.

## Checklist
- Regularly review scope against schedule
- Identify tasks at risk and decide to cut or postpone
- Inform team of cuts and rationale

## Anti-Patterns
- Holding onto features too long
- Not making tough decisions
