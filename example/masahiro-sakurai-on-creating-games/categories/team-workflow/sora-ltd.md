# The Story of Sora Ltd.

Category: Team Management & Workflow

## Summary
Sora Ltd. is Sakurai's personal company for contracting. No employees, no hiring, does not accept resumes. Business model: no advance payment, only revenue share.

## Use When
- Considering lean development setup or independent contracting.

## Do This
1. If solo developer, consider simple company structure for contracts. Focus on project-based work with revenue share. Don't expand unless necessary.

## Checklist
- Do I need company entity for contracts?
- Can operate without hiring employees?
- Is revenue share sustainable?

## Anti-Patterns
- Accepting unsolicited resumes or applications
- Expanding scope beyond personal capacity
