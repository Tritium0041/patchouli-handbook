# Explain Ideas to Everyone at Once

Category: Team Management & Workflow

## Summary
All team members relevant to a feature gather for a single explanation session, reducing misinterpretation.

## Use When
- Communicating design specs to cross-disciplinary team.

## Do This
1. Schedule joint meetings with all roles. Use large screens and microphones. Encourage questions. Hold regular cross-check meetings.

## Checklist
- Invite all relevant stakeholders to one session
- Prepare visuals and examples
- Keep meetings focused and time-boxed
- Follow up with written summary

## Anti-Patterns
- Cascading info through multiple people
- Holding separate meetings per discipline
