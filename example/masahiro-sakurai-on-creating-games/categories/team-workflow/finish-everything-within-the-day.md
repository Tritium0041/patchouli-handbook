# Finish Everything Within the Day

Category: Team Management & Workflow

## Summary
Manage review requests via email, process in arrival order, reply same day. Dedicate no-review time for personal work.

## Use When
- You are a lead or reviewer facing bottleneck.

## Do This
1. Use email as sole channel for review requests. Process emails FIFO. Reply same day. Block out part of day as no-review time.

## Checklist
- Review requests only via email
- Emails processed FIFO
- Same-day reply
- Dedicated personal work time scheduled
- Team aware of policy

## Anti-Patterns
- Using multiple messaging platforms
- Leaving emails unread for days
- Responding immediately to every notification
