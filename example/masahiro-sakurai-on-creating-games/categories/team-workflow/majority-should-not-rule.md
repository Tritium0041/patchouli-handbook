# Majority Should Not Rule

Category: Team Management & Workflow

## Summary
In creative decisions, majority voting tends to produce safe, mediocre results. A well-informed director should make final calls.

## Use When
- Facing team disagreement on creative direction.

## Do This
1. Collect all perspectives. Ensure decision-maker has full information. Avoid simple voting. If voting, treat as informal poll.

## Checklist
- Gather pros and cons from each side
- Assess decision impact on overall design
- Make decisive call with rationale
- Communicate reasoning to team

## Anti-Patterns
- Letting loudest voices decide
- Using democratic process for artistic judgment
- Delaying decisions to avoid conflict
