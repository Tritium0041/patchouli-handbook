# Decide on the Spot

Category: Team Management & Workflow

## Summary
Don't postpone decisions. In meetings, urge decision-makers to decide immediately unless key person absent.

## Use When
- During meetings or any decision blocking progress.

## Do This
1. Force yourself/others to decide within meeting. Gather info beforehand. If wrong, correct quickly.

## Checklist
- Is decision-maker present? If yes, push
- Collected enough data?
- Allowing perfect to be enemy of good?

## Anti-Patterns
- Saying 'let's take it offline' without deadline
- Continuously delaying
- Deciding and never revisiting
