# Ten People Can Produce Seven People's Work

Category: Team Management & Workflow

## Summary
A 10-person team typically produces only 7 people's work due to management overhead. Experienced members transitioning to leadership reduce personal output but improve team efficiency.

## Use When
- Forming or evaluating team structure.

## Do This
1. Account for management cost. Assign experienced roles to quality control. Hold periodic team presentations.

## Checklist
- Calculate effective team capacity
- Designate clear leadership
- Organize outcome report meetings

## Anti-Patterns
- Assuming linear scalability
- Burdening top talent with too much management
