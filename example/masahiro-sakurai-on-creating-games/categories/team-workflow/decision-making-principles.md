# Decision-Making Principles

Category: Team Management & Workflow

## Summary
In meetings, make decisions immediately. Avoid postponing conclusions even if imperfect; course-correct later if needed.

## Use When
- During team meetings or design discussions.

## Do This
1. Force a decision by end of meeting. If missing info, make provisional decision and plan to revisit. Trust director's judgment.

## Checklist
- Decision reached at meeting end
- Action items recorded
- Follow-up scheduled if provisional

## Anti-Patterns
- Repeatedly deferring decisions without deadline
- Waiting for perfect data before deciding
