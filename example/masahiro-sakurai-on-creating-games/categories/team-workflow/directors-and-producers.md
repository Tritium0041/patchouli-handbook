# Directors and Producers

Category: Team Management & Workflow

## Summary
Director oversees content decisions; producer manages team, budget, schedule, marketing. Both must collaborate.

## Use When
- Setting up development team or clarifying roles.

## Do This
1. Respect boundaries: director final on creative, producer on logistics. Communicate regularly, share goals, balance constraints with quality.

## Checklist
- Roles clearly defined
- Communication channels open
- Director has creative authority
- Producer ensures resources
- Both committed to same vision

## Anti-Patterns
- Micromanaging other's domain
- Director ignoring budget/schedule
- Producer overriding creative without reason
