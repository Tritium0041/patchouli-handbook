# Role of Director vs Producer

Category: Team Management & Workflow

## Summary
Director owns game content and has final say on creative. Producer handles budget, schedule, team management, external relations.

## Use When
- Clarifying responsibilities in a project.

## Do This
1. Director makes judgment on design, art, features. Producer manages team health, budget, communication with upper management.

## Checklist
- Director empowered to make final creative decisions
- Producer handles non-creative logistics
- Both communicate regularly

## Anti-Patterns
- Producer overruling creative decisions
- Director ignoring budget or schedule
