# Don't Rely on a 'Plan B'

Category: Team Management & Workflow

## Summary
In design documents, propose the single best solution you've arrived at, then refine through team feedback.

## Use When
- Writing game design docs or pitches.

## Do This
1. Identify ideal solution based on expertise. Present only that solution with reasoning. Be open to feedback but maintain core vision.

## Checklist
- Thoroughly considered alternatives internally?
- Proposal clear and justified?
- Ready to defend while listening?
- Update plan after discussion

## Anti-Patterns
- Including 'or we could also do X' as safety net
- Letting feedback dilute core idea
- Creating options to avoid commitment
