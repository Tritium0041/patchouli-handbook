# Company-Supported Game Development

Category: Team Management & Workflow

## Summary
Working within a company provides stable salary, shared risk, and support. Recognize the value of organizational infrastructure.

## Use When
- Evaluating corporate vs independent development.

## Do This
1. Leverage company resources for learning and tools. Adhere to rules and support colleagues. Understand you are part of support system.

## Checklist
- Provide necessary training for new hires
- Invest in middleware and license management
- Facilitate cross-department communication

## Anti-Patterns
- Treating team members as expendable
- Ignoring corporate support
- Avoiding collaboration
