# Up is Down, Down is Up

Category: Team Management & Workflow

## Summary
As a director or lead, you are not above the team; you are requesting their help. Treat everyone with respect and speak politely.

## Use When
- Leading a team.

## Do This
1. Always use polite language. Remember team members are partners. Be humble and grateful.

## Checklist
- Use respectful language in all communications
- Ask, don't demand
- Acknowledge each person's role and effort publicly

## Anti-Patterns
- Ordering people around
- Taking credit for others' work
