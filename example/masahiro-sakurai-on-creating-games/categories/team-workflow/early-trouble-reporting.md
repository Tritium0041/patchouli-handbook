# Early Trouble Reporting

Category: Team Management & Workflow

## Summary
Encourage team members to report problems as early as possible, so director can adjust the plan.

## Use When
- During development, especially mid-phase.

## Do This
1. Create culture where raising red flag is safe. Hold regular check-ins. If stuck, help re-evaluate or reassign.

## Checklist
- Establish clear process for escalating issues
- Hold brief daily/weekly status meetings
- Make clear early warnings valued not punished

## Anti-Patterns
- Punishing people for reporting delays
- Ignoring early signs of trouble
