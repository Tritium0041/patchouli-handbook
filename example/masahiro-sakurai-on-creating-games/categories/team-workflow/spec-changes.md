# Spec Changes

Category: Team Management & Workflow

## Summary
Avoid spec changes when possible, but be flexible; when you find a problem, report immediately and decide quickly with full information.

## Use When
- Facing a crossroads decision.

## Do This
1. Plan thoroughly early. When discovering issue, inform team right away. Gather data quickly. Make decision without delay.

## Checklist
- Reported issue immediately
- Gathered necessary information
- Made decision swiftly
- Evaluated costs and benefits

## Anti-Patterns
- Delaying decision hoping problem resolves itself
- Changing specs without understanding consequences
- Hiding problems
