# Lessons from Mr. Iwata

Category: Team Management & Workflow

## Summary
Satoru Iwata's leadership: hands-on problem-solving, open communication, service-oriented spirit.

## Use When
- Seeking leadership inspiration.

## Do This
1. Personally step in to solve critical bugs. Hold one-on-one meetings. Use fun presentations to connect with community. Maintain positive attitude.

## Checklist
- Reserve time for individual conversations with each team member
- If technical crisis, consider jumping in
- Communicate company vision engagingly

## Anti-Patterns
- Being detached from team
- Only managing without understanding technical challenges
