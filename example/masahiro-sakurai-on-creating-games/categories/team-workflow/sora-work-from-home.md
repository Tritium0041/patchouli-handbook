# Sora's Work-From-Home Strategies

Category: Team Management & Workflow

## Summary
When Smash Ultimate switched to remote work, issues included poor desks, slow internet. Solution: give each employee budget to choose own equipment. Expect 30% productivity drop.

## Use When
- Setting up remote work policy.

## Do This
1. Provide flexible budget for remote workers to purchase essential equipment. Let them choose and own items. Regularly check ergonomic needs.

## Checklist
- Survey team for specific equipment needs
- Set per-person budget
- Create order process
- Allow employees to keep equipment
- Plan for reduced overhead

## Anti-Patterns
- Assuming everyone has adequate home setups
- Not compensating for extra costs
