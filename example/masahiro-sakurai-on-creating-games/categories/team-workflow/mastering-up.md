# Mastering Up

Category: Team Management & Workflow

## Summary
Master Up is the final delivery milestone. It's not always celebratory; often involves tough compromises. Acknowledge effort and accept imperfections.

## Use When
- Approaching game completion and release.

## Do This
1. Recognize team's hard work; accept no game is perfect; if possible, plan post-launch updates.

## Checklist
- Plan final delivery process
- Prepare for mixed emotions
- Celebrate achievements regardless

## Anti-Patterns
- Obsessing over perfection at last minute
- Neglecting emotional aspect of release
